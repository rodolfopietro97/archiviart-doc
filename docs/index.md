# Archiviart

Archiviart is a digital infrastructure for the art world designed to organize,
protect, connect and make art-related information more accessible and reliable.

It is built as a modular ecosystem. The initial product focus is on three
pillars, with the other modules acting as supporting infrastructure:

| Pillar | Module | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certification | **Archiviart Certifier** (CEDiA) | Collects evidence about artworks and objects, produces dossiers and certificates, anchors evidence on blockchain |
| Professionals | **Archiviart Expertise** | Registry (and future social network) of art professionals |
| Prices | **Archiviart Pulse** | Market intelligence built from real market observations |

Supporting modules: **Docs** (documents and evidence), **Workspace**
(operational environment for galleries and institutions), **Showcase** (public
presentation), **Market** (transactions) and **Mecenate** (artist funding).
